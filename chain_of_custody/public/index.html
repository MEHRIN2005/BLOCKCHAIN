Demo script
  1. Act as Police, type some text, click Register.
  2. Verify tab: same text gives Authentic. Change one letter and it gives Tampered.
  3. Transfer tab: Police requests a transfer to the Forensic Lab.
  4. Switch to Police and try to approve it. It is rejected: only a supervisor can.
  5. Switch to Supervisor and approve. Switch to Forensic Lab and accept.
  6. Share tab: the new custodian grants the Court HASH-only access. The Court uses it, but FILE access is denied.
  7. History tab shows every step, in order.
